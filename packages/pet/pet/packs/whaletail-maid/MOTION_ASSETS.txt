Built-in Dafeiyu motion assets

Asset terms: the whale-tail maid character and its animations come from
PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet (https://github.com/PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet). Its README
states that assets are for non-commercial use only and that derivative works
must credit that address wherever they are introduced, shown, or distributed.
UPSTREAM_ASSET_TERMS.md quotes those terms verbatim with the pinned commits.
MOTION_LICENSE.txt and LICENSE preserve the upstream MIT text, which covers
code only.

Sixteen of the .webm files are cropped and re-encoded from qcytsn/dsh-dafeiyu's
whale-tail maid frames, which derive from PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet animations.
dsh-dafeiyu labels those frames MIT; this pack follows the more restrictive
upstream asset terms instead. motion.json records the pinned repository
revision, source video hashes, frame counts, durations and output hashes, plus
the upstream merge baseline (upstreamBaseline) at which the 14 source video
hashes were checked again. Videos retain the original 412x344 frame
resolution.

Four more .webm files (click_bounce, click_shy, click_huff, look_around) are
the first batch cut directly from PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet's in-repository
assets/webm at commit 091b27b40f9eeecc814bedb691fe214ec2b48299. The upstream
green-screen source videos are only published in a release archive and were
not used. ../../tools/import-dshpet-webm.py reads the recipe in
../../tools/dshpet-webm.recipe.json, crops the shared 412x344 window without
scaling, and re-encodes with the same VP9 alpha settings as the other clips.
motion.json records each source path, SHA-256, frame count, crop, and the
generation stated upstream: 豆包 (Doubao), manual Premiere Pro keying, and the
prompt section in the upstream prompt file. look_around ends mirrored, so it
is marked as a turn.

Three more .webm files (crab_walk, float_step, turn_run) are the second batch,
cut the same way from the upstream walking clips 螃蟹走路, 原地漂浮踏步 and
原地左转奔跑. Upstream moves the window evenly while these play; here the
importer measures the travel from the clip itself instead: it decodes the alpha
plane, follows the foot contacts along the bottom of the body from frame to
frame, and records the cumulative travel per frame in source pixels (move.travel)
with the stand-still lead and tail times stated in upstream
assets/config.jsonc. It also records the mean foot slip without and with that
travel. A clip is given a move record only when its contacts agree on one
direction; turn_run turns and runs on the spot, so it has none and plays in
place. The chain weights (idle 10, turn 5, move 5) are upstream's
animationWeights.

Six more .webm files (balance_full, balance_jingle, balance_steady,
balance_frown, balance_low, balance_empty) are the third batch, cut the same
way from the upstream balance clips 余额-钱袋满溢, 余额-金袋叮当, 余额-钱袋如常,
余额-数金皱眉, 余额-袋空如洗 and 余额-分文不剩 (8,415,507 bytes together).
motion.json lists them as the six balance tiers of the upstream
animations.events (used balance in 20% bands, and exactly 100%), together with
the upstream work-status tiers mapped onto the existing state clips. Its
"clicks" entry is a Studio choice: the five upstream click responses split by
the body area that is clicked.

Six more .webm files are the fourth batch, cut the same way from 原地小憩沉眠,
打瞌睡被惊醒, 碎碎念-发呆碎碎念 and 深度思考碎碎念 (4,985,619 bytes together).
原地小憩沉眠 is split by source frame windows into doze_enter (frames 0-71),
doze_loop (72-169, looping; frame 170 differs from frame 72 by 0.8% of alpha)
and doze_wake (170-240); doze_startle is 打瞌睡被惊醒 from frame 60, where
she already dozes, and is the alternative wake-up. motion.json records each
window as source.frames. whisper_daydream joins the small-actions category and
the whisper event; thinking_deep joins the thinking rotation. These names stay
clear of the AI-video slots below, which take precedence when present.

The fifth batch adds the 75 remaining upstream clips, cut the same way
(122,903,599 bytes together): 15 small actions, among them the whisper
variants whisper_wipe_desk and whisper_screen, which join the whisper event;
3 dances, 8 talents and 15 games (categories dance, talent and games, which
share the "fun" cooldown of 3 minutes); 13 meals and snacks (category eat,
with eat_token; 20-minute cooldown); and 21 seasons and festivals (category
festival; 10-minute cooldown). The 14 upstream clips imported earlier through
dsh-dafeiyu are not imported again, so every one of the 106 upstream clips
ships exactly once. The "schedule" entry of motion.json lists when time-bound
clips may play autonomously: meals at local 7-9, 11-13 and 17-19, snacks at
15-17 and 21-23, toothbrushing at 5-9, seasonal clips in their months, and
festival clips while the festival window of src/time-context.ts (adapted
from upstream) covers the date. what_to_eat and write_fu carry text and never
mirror; thinking_deep (upstream category 文字) no longer mirrors either.
19 of the sources draw outside the shared crop window (x 100-511, y 0-343 of
the 640x360 source), so the crop cuts their props, effects or text there; by
opaque pixels outside the window, most first: mooncake, what_to_eat,
rocking_horse, fireworks, butterfly_bloom, water_gun, balloon,
needle_threading, falling_leaves, animal_friends, snack_munch, christmas_tree,
flute, brush_teeth, hotpot, conjure_flower, dinner, jump_grab, dove_magic.

Fourteen generated PNG sprite sheets (bubbles, celebrate, nuzzle, sleep,
idle_daydream, idle_observe, swim, idle_groom, play_bubbles, play_ball,
play_tailspin, work_read, thinking_chin, rest_pillow) and their 24 clip entries
were removed from this pack, together with their import tool and recipe. The
pack now ships only the 110 transparent .webm clips above plus the fallback
frames. Without those clips the client falls back: rest plays the doze clips;
play draws from click_bounce, click_huff and turn_run; wandering moves only with
the crab_walk and float_step walking clips (no glide); a finished task plays
success; thinking rotates thinking and thinking_deep.

Their action names stay reserved as slots for AI-video clips. A clip imported
with ../../tools/import-ai-video.py under such a name (sleep_enter, sleep_loop,
sleep_wake, pillow_enter, pillow_loop, pillow_wake, swim_start, swim_loop,
swim_stop, celebrate, bubbles, play_ball, ...) is picked up without code
changes; to appear in the menu or in autonomous play it also has to join a
category. motion.json records each such clip with an "ai-video" source: the
source file name and SHA-256, frame rate, crop, the generation tool and prompt,
and the keying and normalization applied. ../../tools/AI_VIDEO_CLIPS.zh.md
describes the steps. AI-video clips that depict the upstream character are
derivative works under the same upstream asset terms: non-commercial use only,
with credit to https://github.com/PC2005-cloud/dsh-pet. No legacy restricted
BigFish assets are included.
The older 64-frame-target draft is not shipped in the motion manifest.
